# Woodgrove — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Woodgrove logo](../logos/generated/woodgrove.webp)

## Visual language

eco-conscious durable design.

## Palette

moss green, walnut, warm gray.

## Materials

recycled matte polymer and wood-toned inserts.

## Form language

soft durable geometry.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Woodgrove product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A simple grove leaf inside a rounded square.
- **Geometry:** soft organic leaf with a calm enclosing frame.
- **Colors:** moss green, walnut, and warm gray.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
