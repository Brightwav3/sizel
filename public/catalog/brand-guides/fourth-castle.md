# Fourth Castle — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Fourth Castle logo](../logos/generated/fourth-castle.webp)

## Visual language

premium living-room console.

## Palette

charcoal, warm gold, deep plum.

## Materials

monolithic satin surfaces and subtle architectural details.

## Form language

quiet architectural volumes.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Fourth Castle product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A minimal castle gate inside a squared architectural frame.
- **Geometry:** monolithic verticals, one centered arch, restrained detail.
- **Colors:** charcoal, warm gold, and deep plum.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
