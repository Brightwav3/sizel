# Acme Labs — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Acme Labs logo](../logos/acme-labs.webp)

## Visual language

practical lab engineering.

## Palette

white, slate, safety lime.

## Materials

brushed metal, exposed fasteners, modular geometry.

## Form language

precise utilitarian shapes.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Acme Labs product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A hexagonal flask mark with one small lime calibration dot.
- **Geometry:** thin slate outline, precise angles, modular grid alignment.
- **Colors:** slate with a safety-lime accent.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
