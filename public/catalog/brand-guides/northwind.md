# Northwind — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Northwind logo](../logos/generated/northwind.webp)

## Visual language

premium compute performance.

## Palette

deep navy, teal, copper.

## Materials

aerodynamic metal, precision vents, technical detailing.

## Form language

wide performance geometry with deliberate vents.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Northwind product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A north-pointing arrow enclosed by an aerodynamic ring.
- **Geometry:** forward-leaning ring with a precise compass point.
- **Colors:** deep navy, teal, and copper.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
