# Proseware — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Proseware logo](../logos/proseware.webp)

## Visual language

data-speed specialist.

## Palette

graphite, electric blue, amber.

## Materials

compact machined metal and status-window details.

## Form language

small dense precision hardware.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Proseware product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** Three stacked data bars forming a compact P shape.
- **Geometry:** rectilinear bars with measured spacing and a small cutout.
- **Colors:** graphite, electric blue, and amber.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
