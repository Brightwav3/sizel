# Adventure Works — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Adventure Works logo](../logos/generated/adventure-works.webp)

## Visual language

rugged outdoor reliability.

## Palette

forest green, sand, safety orange.

## Materials

textured polymer, rubber armor, reinforced corners.

## Form language

durable protected shapes.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Adventure Works product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A compass arrow nested inside a protective shield.
- **Geometry:** bold shield contour with a clear directional arrow.
- **Colors:** forest green with safety-orange accent.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
