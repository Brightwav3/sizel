# Tailspin — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Tailspin logo](../logos/tailspin.webp)

## Visual language

kinetic airflow engineering.

## Palette

gunmetal, black, red-orange.

## Materials

industrial mesh and bold edges.

## Form language

spiraling vent motifs and directional flow.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Tailspin product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** Three curved blades rotating around a small center point.
- **Geometry:** dynamic spiral with three equal directional blades.
- **Colors:** gunmetal, black, and red-orange.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
