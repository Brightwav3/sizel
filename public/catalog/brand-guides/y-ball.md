# Y-Ball — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Y-Ball logo](../logos/generated/y-ball.webp)

## Visual language

playful accessible gaming.

## Palette

bright yellow, cobalt, off-white.

## Materials

smooth friendly plastic and high-contrast accents.

## Form language

rounded approachable shapes.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Y-Ball product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A bright ball with a playful Y-shaped orbit path.
- **Geometry:** rounded circular core and three bouncy diverging arcs.
- **Colors:** bright yellow, cobalt, and off-white.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
