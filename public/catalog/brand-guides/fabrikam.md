# Fabrikam — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Fabrikam logo](../logos/generated/fabrikam.webp)

## Visual language

high-performance gaming.

## Palette

matte black, violet, magenta.

## Materials

angular shells, mesh, controlled accent lighting.

## Form language

aggressive angular gaming geometry.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Fabrikam product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** An angular F built from two interlocking chevrons.
- **Geometry:** aggressive diagonal cuts and compact symmetry.
- **Colors:** matte black with violet and magenta accents.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
