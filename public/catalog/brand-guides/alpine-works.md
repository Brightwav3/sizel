# Alpine Works — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Alpine Works logo](../logos/generated/alpine-works.webp)

## Visual language

quiet thermal precision.

## Palette

ice blue, graphite, silver.

## Materials

frosted metal, dense fins, cool smooth curves.

## Form language

thermal fins and calm rounded engineering.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Alpine Works product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A layered mountain peak crossed by two clean airflow lines.
- **Geometry:** quiet triangular peak with parallel technical curves.
- **Colors:** ice blue, graphite, and silver.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
