# Pear — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Pear logo](../logos/generated/pear.webp)

## Visual language

minimal consumer elegance.

## Palette

white, silver, soft pastel accent.

## Materials

seamless rounded ceramic-like surfaces.

## Form language

simple smooth slabs and unbroken edges.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Pear product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A single abstract pear silhouette with one tiny leaf.
- **Geometry:** seamless rounded silhouette with no internal clutter.
- **Colors:** silver with one soft pastel accent.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
