# Litware — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Litware logo](../logos/generated/litware.webp)

## Visual language

slim mobile innovation.

## Palette

white, cyan, coral.

## Materials

glass and satin aluminum.

## Form language

thin rounded slabs and precise seams.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Litware product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** A thin lightning leaf combining a soft curve and one sharp fold.
- **Geometry:** slim continuous line with a crisp central break.
- **Colors:** white, cyan, and coral.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
