# Contoso — image style manual

## Brand mark

![Contoso logo](../logos/generated/contoso.webp)

## Visual language

friendly mainstream technology.

## Palette

navy, cobalt, silver.

## Materials

clean molded plastic and satin metal.

## Form language

balanced geometric surfaces.

## Image direction

Use this style for every Contoso product. Let the product specifications control the visible ports, slots, cooling, proportions, materials, and controls.

## Hard rules

1. Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio background.
2. Phones are product only; never include a phone box.
3. Other products may show the product, its box, or both.
4. No text, logo, label, watermark, diagram, or floating typography anywhere except text physically printed on the product or its box.
5. Use English text only when physical product or box text is necessary.

## Logo

- **Mark:** Four rounded squares orbiting a small central point.
- **Geometry:** friendly balanced grid with soft corners.
- **Colors:** navy, cobalt, and silver.
- **Placement:** Use the logo only as a physical print, emboss, or small device mark on the product or its box. Never place a logo or text in the background.
